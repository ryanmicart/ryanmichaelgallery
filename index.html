Ryan Michael

I am an Australian portrait painter working within a realist tradition, focused on cultural identity, history, and place. My practice centres on portraiture as a means of representing underrepresented communities both locally and globally. Through careful observation and detail, I explore themes of race, politics, belonging, and lived experience across diverse social and geographic contexts.

I completed a Master's in Applied Design and Art at Curtin University, with a focus on photojournalism. My research thesis examined the conflict in Myanmar, grounding my practice in documentary observation and narrative image-making. I later undertook a degree in Education with a major in Visual Art. While my academic training established a strong foundation in visual communication and teaching, my photographic practice was shaped through apprenticeship with renowned French-Senegalese photographer and artist Mr. Bamba Surang, undertaken in Mauritius.

My early work in street photography developed from this foundation and has since evolved into a sustained focus on painted portraiture, built through independent study, observation, and long-term commitment to realist practice. Having lived and worked across Africa, Asia, Europe, and the Americas, my practice has been shaped by direct engagement with a wide range of communities and contexts.

Across these experiences, I have observed recurring threads of inequality and resilience that transcend geography, particularly in relation to visibility and representation. My experience as an immigrant in Australia has also shaped my understanding of identity and belonging, creating a more layered awareness of what it means to be "Australian" both within Australia and in an international context.

Rather than idealising or simplifying, I aim to present subjects with clarity and honesty, inviting viewers into a direct and human exchange. At the core of my practice is a belief that portraiture can expand how we see one another — contributing to a more inclusive representation of contemporary Australia and beyond, and encouraging reflection on identity, belonging, and shared human experience.

FAQ

Do you accept commissions?

Yes. Use the form above to describe what you're looking for — size, subject, colour palette — and I'll provide a quote and timeline.

What are the prints printed on?

310gsm Hahnemühle fine art paper using archival pigment inks. Each print is signed, numbered, and comes with a certificate of authenticity.

Do I receive a certificate of authenticity?

Yes. Every original and limited edition print comes with a signed certificate confirming title, medium, dimensions, edition number, and date.

Standard prints vs. limited editions?

Standard prints are always available in A4, A3, and A2 on archival paper. Limited editions are restricted to 25 per image — signed, numbered, with certificate. Once sold out, no more are produced.

Can I see a work in person before buying?

Studio visits by appointment if you're local. Otherwise I'm happy to provide additional photos or arrange a video call.

What payment methods do you accept?

Credit/debit cards via Stripe. For originals, bank transfer and payment plans are also available — just ask.
